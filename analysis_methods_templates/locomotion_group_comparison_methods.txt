Locomotion metrics compared across viral groups

Overview
- Tests whether the three viral groups differ in how much the animals actually navigate.
- This is the premise check for the whole navigation-aware suite. The concern motivating that
  suite is that group differences in the cell-averaged spike rate could reflect group differences in
  locomotion rather than a cell-intrinsic effect of the DREADD manipulation. If the groups do not
  differ in locomotion, that explanation is ruled out directly. If they do, the movement-restricted
  rate panels and the rate-vs-locomotion ANCOVA are what adjust for it.

Inputs
- Session dictionaries: TFC_cond, Test_A, Test_A_1wk, Test_B, Test_B_1wk, LT1, LT2.
- Per-frame smoothed speed (velocities_miniscope_smooth, cm/s), derived from the ezTrack
  LocationOutput behaviour-camera tracking, resampled to miniscope frames by nearest timestamp
  and Gaussian-smoothed. This is the same trace the occupancy analysis consumes, so the numbers
  are directly comparable with the occupancy panels.

Metrics
- distance_cm            total path length over the window, computed as sum(speed) * frame duration.
- mean_speed_cms         mean speed over all frames in the window, including immobile frames.
- mean_speed_moving_cms  mean speed over frames with speed >= 2 cm/s only.
- pct_time_moving        percentage of frames in the window with speed >= 2 cm/s.
- The last two decompose the first two: mean_speed_cms is approximately the product of how fast
  the animal moves when it moves and how often it moves. Separating them distinguishes an animal
  that runs fast in short bouts from one that ambles continuously, which the total distance alone
  cannot.
- The 2 cm/s threshold is VELOCITY_THRESHOLD, the same criterion that defines S_mov / S_imm and
  the immobility score in the occupancy analysis.

Analysis windows
- whole_session: the entire trimmed recording, matching the whole-session spike-rate panels.
- pretone_180s: the pre-tone period, taken as the first tone onset where tones exist and the
  first 180 s otherwise. This is the window used by the occupancy analysis and the 2D
  spatial-information suite. LT1 / LT2 have no tones and receive only whole_session.

Statistics
- The unit of analysis is the MOUSE. Each animal contributes one value per metric, shown as a
  violin with individual animals overlaid. Groups are compared by one-way ANOVA
  (scipy.stats.f_oneway); only if the omnibus p < 0.05 is a Tukey HSD post-hoc test run and
  significant pairs annotated. n = number of mice per group.
- Groups are built from the mice actually present in each session dictionary (G07 absent from
  Test_B; G15 absent from Test_A_1wk / Test_B_1wk).
- Group order is hM3D, mCherry, hM4D (displayed as Exc, Ctl, Inh).
- A non-significant omnibus test here is evidence of absence only to the extent the design is
  powered to detect a difference; the accompanying per-mouse table reports the group means and
  the full per-animal values so the effect size can be judged rather than inferred from p alone.

Why these metrics are reported separately rather than divided out of the firing rates
- Firing rates elsewhere in this suite are NOT normalized by speed or by distance travelled. That
  is deliberate. The rate/speed relation has a substantial nonzero intercept, so a rate/speed
  ratio diverges as speed approaches zero and is uninterpretable, and it would break comparability
  with every published firing rate. The standard treatment of immobility in the hippocampal
  literature is RESTRICTION -- a running-speed threshold applied identically to spike counts and
  to occupancy -- not normalization, because the hippocampus occupies two distinct network states
  (theta during locomotion, large-irregular-activity/sharp-wave-ripple during immobility) with
  qualitatively different firing statistics. Averaging across them mixes two phenomena rather than
  averaging out a confound.
- This figure is therefore a PREMISE CHECK, not a correction: it establishes whether there is any
  locomotion difference for the firing-rate comparisons to be confounded by. The movement-
  restricted panels in place_cell_rates/ are the restriction, and rate_vs_locomotion/ is the
  covariate adjustment.
- The metrics here are group MEANS. Two groups can share a mean speed while distributing their
  time across speeds differently, and this figure cannot see that. The speed-binned analysis in
  navigation_aware_single_cell/speed_tuning/ tests the distributions directly, and reweights the
  rates to a common speed distribution (direct standardization) rather than dividing by speed.

Outputs
- PLOTS_DIR/navigation_aware_single_cell/locomotion_metrics/<window>/
    locomotion_group_comparison-<session_type>.png/.svg
    locomotion_summary.csv        (tidy per-mouse table: mouse, group, session, all metrics)
    locomotion_group_stats.txt    (per-session, per-metric ANOVA F and p, with group means)
