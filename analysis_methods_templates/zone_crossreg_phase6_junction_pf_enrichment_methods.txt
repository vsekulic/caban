Phase 6: Junction place-field enrichment & coverage
====================================================

Rationale
---------
Phase 1 showed that the improvement in LT1→LT2 cross-session decoding
at the junction when using triple cross-registration (LT1+LT2+TFC)
is not attributable to cell count (N-matched random subsamples do not
reproduce the effect).  Per-cell rate-map stability (tested previously)
did not distinguish triple-set from pair-only cells.

Phase 6 instead tests a *population-level* hypothesis: does the
triple intersection selectively enrich for cells that have place
fields at the junction?  If so, the decoder would have more
informative neurons covering the junction, improving accuracy
specifically in that zone.

Method
------
For each mouse, all cells mapped across LT1+LT2 (pair set) are
identified.  Each cell is labelled "included" (present also in
TFC_cond, i.e. the triple set) or "excluded" (pair-only).

1D occupancy-corrected rate maps are computed for LT1 and LT2
(50 bins, Gaussian smooth σ = 2.0 bins, speed ≥ 2 cm/s).

Five-zone boundaries are detected from the track geometry
(_compute_5_zone_boundaries); the junction zone spans turn1 to turn2.

Part A — Junction place-field enrichment (per-cell)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A cell is classified as having a "junction place field" if the peak
bin of its 1D rate map (in either LT1 or LT2) falls within the
junction zone.

For each group, a 2×2 contingency table is constructed:
  (triple vs pair-only) × (junction PF vs no junction PF)
and tested with Fisher's exact test (two-sided).  The odds ratio
quantifies how much more likely a junction-coding cell is to survive
triple cross-registration.

Part B — Population junction coverage (bin-level, N-matched)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
For each cell, "significant bins" are those where the 1D rate exceeds
the cell's mean rate.  For each mouse, the fraction of junction bins
covered by ≥1 cell is computed separately for triple-set and pair-only
subsets.

To control for unequal N, the larger subset is randomly subsampled
to match the smaller (200 repeats, mean taken).  Per-mouse paired
coverage fractions are compared with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test
(per group).

Output
------
- zone_stability_enrichment.csv: per-cell zone-specific correlations
- zone_stability_enrichment_boxplot.{png,pdf}: grouped boxplots
- zone_stability_enrichment_delta.{png,pdf}: enrichment-delta bars
- zone_stability_interaction_anova.txt: ANOVA table (if run)
