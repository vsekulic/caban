METHODS — Phase 7: Zone-Resolved Population Vector Correlation Comparison
=========================================================================

Purpose
-------
Test whether triple-set cells (cross-registered across LT1 + LT2 + TFC_cond)
produce higher LT1→LT2 population-vector (PV) correlation at the track junction
compared with excluded (pair-only) cells, and whether this difference is
group-dependent.

Cell identification
-------------------
For each mouse, all LT1+LT2 pair-registered cells are split into:
  • Triple-set: cells also registered in TFC_cond (via mappings_crossreg_1.csv)
  • Excluded: pair-only cells (LT1+LT2 but NOT in TFC_cond mapping)

Tuning matrix construction
--------------------------
For each cell subset (triple, excluded) independently:
  1. Extract S (deconvolved activity) rows for the subset from LT1 and LT2.
  2. Compute spatial tuning curves: mean S per spatial bin (120 linearly spaced
     bins spanning the shared LT1+LT2 track range).
  3. Smooth with a 3-bin boxcar kernel.
  4. Pairwise per-cell normalization: for each cell, compute the 95th percentile
     across both LT1 and LT2 tuning vectors; divide both by the maximum of the
     two (shared scale factor per cell).

PV correlation matrix
---------------------
For each subset, compute an n_bins × n_bins matrix where entry [i, j] is the
Pearson correlation between the population vector at bin i in LT1 and bin j in
LT2. A population vector at bin k is the vector of all cell tuning values at
that bin (one element per cell in the subset).

Zone splitting
--------------
Using the average of LT1 and LT2 turn points (arm-detection percentile method):
  • arm_A: bins with centre < turn1
  • joint: bins with turn1 ≤ centre ≤ turn2
  • arm_B: bins with centre > turn2
For each zone, the corresponding square sub-matrix is extracted and diagonal
metrics computed (diag_mean, diagonal_excess, specificity_idx, decode_acc,
band_conc_k2).

Statistical tests
-----------------
A) Paired comparison (within-group):
   For each group, Wilcoxon signed-rank on the mouse-level paired difference
   (triple − excluded) of junction diag_mean.

B) Between-group comparison:
   Kruskal–Wallis H test on the Δ (triple − excluded) junction diag_mean
   across three groups.

Output
------
  • zone_pv_corr_comparison.csv — per (mouse, subset, zone) metrics
  • pv_corr_paired_{zone}.png/pdf — paired boxplots per group
  • pv_corr_delta_{zone}.png/pdf — between-group Δ boxplot
