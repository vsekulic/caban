Population coupling / co-activation structure
=============================================

Overview
--------
This analysis measures population synchrony — a correlational axis independent of
single-cell rate — and compares it across the three viral groups (hM3D/Exc,
hM4D/Inh, mCherry/Ctl). Even when per-cell event rates and amplitudes match, an
excitatory/inhibitory DREADD manipulation can change how coordinated cells are.

Neural signal
-------------
For each mouse, session, and mapping, the activity matrix for the mapping's cells —
the denoised calcium trace C by default (deconvolved S selectable via a parameter),
cells x frames — is binned in time by summing within fixed-width
bins (bin width = the pipeline's bin_width_frames), giving a cells x bins matrix.
Cells with zero variance across bins are excluded from correlations (undefined).

Metrics
-------
1. Mean pairwise correlation (global synchrony): the mean of the upper-triangle of
   the cell x cell Pearson correlation matrix of binned activity. One scalar per
   mouse.
2. Per-cell population coupling (Okun-style): for each cell, the Pearson
   correlation of its binned activity with the leave-one-out population sum
   (total minus that cell). A per-cell distribution per mouse.
3. Epoch-restricted synchrony (conditioning session only): metric 1 recomputed on
   frames restricted to each task epoch (pre_tone, tone, trace, shock; windows from
   get_epoch_frames), to test whether coordination is event-specific.

Group comparison
----------------
- Mean pairwise correlation: violin + per-mouse strip across groups, with significant
  differences annotated by pairwise Welch t-tests, Holm-corrected across the three
  contrasts (brackets + stars; not gated on an omnibus ANOVA).
- Per-cell population coupling: group ECDF plus a cell-nested-in-mouse linear mixed
  model (reference group mCherry/Ctl); a mouse-clustered OLS is substituted, and
  noted, if the mixed model fails to converge.
- Epoch-restricted synchrony: one violin panel per epoch (encoding only).

Group coding: hM3D red (Exc), hM4D blue (Inh), mCherry grey (Ctl).

Outputs
-------
Saved under <PLOTS_DIR>/population_coupling/<family>/ where <family> is encoding,
recall_testA, or recall_testB:
- population_coupling-<family>.png/.svg          (synchrony violin + coupling ECDF)
- epoch_synchrony-<family>.png/.svg              (encoding only)
- stats/population_coupling_mixed_model-<family>.txt
- tables/per_mouse_coupling-<family>.csv
